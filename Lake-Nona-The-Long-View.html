01The people and purpose behind the placeWho Tavistock is—and where Isleworth fits into the story.

Tavistock Group is a private international investment organization founded by British businessman Joe Lewis in 1975. Its interests span real estate, hospitality, agriculture and financial services. Tavistock Development Company is the development business behind Lake Nona.[1][2][17]

The Central Florida connection began with a personal interest: Lewis purchased an Isleworth homesite in 1986. Tavistock acquired Isleworth in 1993, then Lake Nona Golf & Country Club and surrounding acreage in 1996.[2][3]

Both golf communities predated Tavistock’s ownership. Isleworth’s course was originally designed by Arnold Palmer; Lake Nona’s golf community dates to 1986. Tavistock’s role was to acquire and build on those foundations, with Lake Nona eventually developing into a much broader community.[18][19]

The owner’s perspective

The relevant history is one of sustained involvement. Tavistock’s connection to Isleworth now spans more than three decades, and its Lake Nona involvement approximately 30 years. Both remain listed among its investments.[20]

02The gift that helped establish Medical CityYes—the UCF land donation is an important part of the history.

In 2005, Tavistock pledged 50 acres and $12.5 million to help establish UCF’s medical college in Lake Nona. A December 2006 university letter reported that subsequent commitments, including state matching funds, exceeded $100 million to support medical college facilities.[5]

When UCF celebrated the medical education building’s opening in 2010, it recognized the Tavistock Foundation’s land donation and challenge grant. Tavistock Green, the campus lawn, carries that history in its name.[4]

The original gift supported the medical school and Health Sciences Campus. The later UCF Lake Nona Hospital was a separate UCF–HCA partnership and opened in March 2021.[6]

Why this matters

A medical and educational district creates enduring reasons for people to work, study and visit nearby. That can support housing demand over time. It does not set the rental or resale value of an individual property.

Lake Nona’s growth also reflects investment by public agencies, universities, healthcare organizations and other private businesses. Tavistock helped establish the setting; many partners helped give it substance.

04How continued involvement supports a communityThe evidence behind the long-term approach.

An October 2026 Commercial Property Executive report provides a concrete example: Tavistock continued an office development despite the difficult post-pandemic office market, funding it itself. The building was completed in 2025 and secured Siemens Energy as an anchor tenant.[2]

Its ongoing community involvement also extends to offices, hospitality, restaurants, public spaces and programming. Town Center brings together those uses with destinations such as Boxi Park, the Wave Hotel and the Lake Nona Performance Club.[13]

05What is open—and what comes nextCompleted investments, announced plans and the distinctions that matter.
Open • phased expansion

Lake Nona West

The 405,000-square-foot, 54-acre center is open. The developer confirmed Target, Homesense, Barnes & Noble and Total Wine were operating by July 2026, with further openings announced into early 2027.[11]

Owner relevance: more everyday services nearby, plus a new destination for shopping and gathering.

Announced • 2027

Siemens Energy

The company plans to move its Orlando offices into more than 242,000 square feet at 6876 Marwick Lane. The move accommodates its nearly 3,000 local employees and a hybrid work model.[12]

Owner relevance: a major employment presence closer to Lake Nona homes and businesses.

Long-term plan

Town Center’s buildout

Tavistock’s current project page describes 4 million square feet of planned retail, restaurant and entertainment space. This is a development vision, not a verified completion schedule.[13]

Owner relevance: further potential amenities, accompanied by construction, traffic and a changing local environment.

Open • since 2025

UCF nursing education

The Dr. Phillips Nursing Pavilion is already open, with capacity intended to help UCF graduate 150 additional newly licensed nurses annually.[10]

Owner relevance: continued educational activity alongside Medical City’s healthcare institutions.

Read employment announcements carefully

Siemens Energy’s move largely relocates existing Orlando employees. It does not mean 3,000 new households will move into Lake Nona. Some employees may choose to live closer; others will commute from their existing homes.

Project plans can change. Disney’s proposed Lake Nona campus was canceled in 2023. For property decisions, give completed projects and confirmed commitments more weight than early announcements.[14]

06Three details worth knowingThe art, the tennis connection and the conversations behind the community.

The art has a family connection.

The 50,000-square-foot sculpture garden beside the Wave Hotel includes works from the Lewis Collection, connecting a visible part of Lake Nona’s cultural identity to the family behind Tavistock.[9]

UCF was not the only institution supported with land.

The USTA land contribution is another example of using land to attract an institution with a long-term presence. The reported $40 million figure is a historical valuation, not a current appraisal.[3]

Lake Nona hosts an ongoing health innovation conversation.

The Lake Nona Impact Forum convenes leaders in health and innovation. Tavistock’s Rasesh Thakkar is a co-founder, and the next announced gathering is February 24–26, 2027.[15]

07What this means for your propertyA promising community and a realistic property strategy belong together.

A community can have strong long-term prospects while an individual owner faces a challenging market today.

Continued investment can improve daily life and give more people reasons to consider the area. At the same time, additional housing gives buyers and renters more choices. Both forces can operate together.

  • If you live in your home: weigh the conveniences you actually use, your expected time in the property and your own financial flexibility.
  • If you rent it out: employment and amenities provide useful context, but available competing rentals, condition, access and price determine how your home competes now.
  • If you are considering selling: future development belongs in the broader picture. Recent comparable sales and current alternatives remain the basis for a realistic pricing strategy.
For owners throughout the corridor

This guide concerns Tavistock’s master-planned Lake Nona community and its wider influence. A “Lake Nona” address or marketing description does not by itself establish that Tavistock developed your subdivision.
